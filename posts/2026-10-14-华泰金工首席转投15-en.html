Huatai's Quant Chief Jumps to a ¥15B Fund Owned by a Gaming Company

2026-10-14 · Ken Zhang / Gravitas Recruitment

He Kang, Huatai Securities' chief of strategy and financial-engineering research, joined Shanghai's Boke Private Fund as Chief Investment Officer on Oct 8. AMAC public records show he obtained his fund-practitioner qualification on Sept 17 and was registered under Boke. A Peking University psychology PhD who spent years on the sell side leading Huatai's AI research series, now managing money for a ¥10B+ fund backed by a casual-games company.

Start with what Boke actually is. Founded in July 2022 and registered in February 2023, it's a platform-style, multi-strategy manager spanning FOF, discretionary long-only, quant and macro. The official figure is total AUM above ¥15 billion, of which proprietary strategies exceed ¥3 billion, with 32 full-time staff.

Thirty-two people running ¥15 billion — roughly ¥470 million per head. That density is not low for the quant world. More telling is the ownership: Boke Technology Group holds 92%, the remaining 8% a management consultancy. Who is Boke Technology? The maker of casual titles like "爆炒江湖" (a cooking sim) and "跃动小子". A gaming company, using game-business cash flow, to fund a ¥10B+ quant shop.

He Kang's CV has two lines worth separating. One is academic: a PhD in psychology from Peking University. Psychology doctorates are not a common profile in quant, but he turned the "judgment and decision-making" foundation into strategy research and product design. The other is sell-side: Huatai Research's assistant head, strategy chief and financial-engineering chief, running AI, quant strategy and asset-allocation research for years, leading Huatai's AI series and co-founding the China Quantitative Investment White Paper.

Stack those two lines and it's clear what Boke hired him for — not to write research notes, but to bring AI methods and asset-allocation experience into its product lines. Boke's own announcement is blunt: He's AI research will give the platform methodological and tooling support, extending new technology across products.

He Kang stated his own read on the industry at Huatai's autumn conference in early September: industry scale expansion and strategy homogenization have crowded quant trading, and momentum collapse drove a visible drawdown this year; traditional alpha competition will only intensify, and macro quant plus multi-asset beta allocation will become the main direction. Translated into recruiter's language: the pure-alpha dividend is narrowing, and money will move toward allocation and beta.

Boke hiring He Kang fits because its strategy shelf already has macro and FOF legs, needing someone who understands broad asset allocation to pull them together. This is supply matching demand, not hiring someone to stand on stage.

He is not the first sell-side quant chief to cross over this year, but he chose a different route from his predecessors. In June 2026, Lin Xiaoming — Huatai's financial-engineering chief for a decade — joined Sirui Investment as chief economist, running a ¥2-5B book focused on global asset allocation. In 2025, Ding Luming, with Haitong and CSC Financial backgrounds, struck out on his own with Shanghai Ruicheng Private Fund — two products filed, still in the ¥0-500M range.

Three routes side by side: Lin went to a mature shop as chief economist; Ding built from zero; He took the CIO seat at a ¥10B+ platform owned by a gaming company. Three people, three risk appetites, three degrees of difficulty in the sell-side-to-buy-side transition. He's biggest variable is the one the others don't have: switching to buy-side live trading.

The line worth remembering from Jiemian's interview: a sell-side financial-engineering analyst's research lives in notes and roadshows, aimed at explaining the market and outputting communicable logic; a quant private-fund researcher's work all revolves around live trading, aimed at digging out deployable alpha that sustains excess returns — transaction costs, strategy capacity, live risk. Two different logics. A chief crossing over has to clear that identity switch first.
