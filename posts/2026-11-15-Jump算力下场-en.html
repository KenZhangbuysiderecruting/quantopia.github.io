Jump Steps Into Compute: 2 Market Makers Back a New Asset

2026-11-15 · Ken Zhang / Gravitas Recruitment

GPU hours priced, hedged and held like crude oil — market makers have started building it. Per Bloomberg in late October, Jump Trading and DRW are committing traders and capital to markets for compute-linked financial contracts. Jump has already formed a dedicated trading team and will take positions on its own balance sheet, bearing the associated risk. With the AI boom under market scrutiny, compute is taking its first step from infrastructure toward a tradeable asset.

Buy the Team First, Open the Market Second

Jump's approach came in two steps. Step one, disclosed mid-October: a new internal compute-trading desk, plus an acqui-hire of Ito Markets founding team members Affaan Mustafa and Alejandro Soumah Fernandez, terms undisclosed. This was not an ordinary quant-researcher move — Ito Markets itself was built to trade compute, so the acquisition imports an entire playbook that has already been run.

Step two, per the late-October follow-up: the team will hold positions on the firm's own balance sheet and bear the risk, across three planned venues — OTC, perpetual contracts and prediction markets. DRW is committing traders and capital in the same direction. Taking the risk onto the balance sheet signals a market-making business line, not a research project.

Why Price Compute at All

The backdrop is the sheer scale of AI capital expenditure. Morgan Stanley projects up to $15 trillion of cumulative AI spending over the next decade — a volume of financing, hedging and risk transfer that capital markets have to absorb. GPU procurement cycles are long and prices volatile; power costs for data-center expansion are uncertain. Standardize compute into contracts, and the buyer locks in cost, the seller earns a risk premium, and the spread in between is the market maker's business.

The hard part is not the model — it is the underlying: unlike crude oil, compute has no unified futures contract or delivery standard. That is what makes the disclosures interesting when read together: Jump listing OTC, perpetuals and prediction markets side by side amounts to testing which contract structure standardizes first. Whoever sets the market structure early holds the position early oil-futures markets occupied.

What It Means for Job Seekers

Compute trading is a new business line, and staffing it is a practical problem. From what has been disclosed, Jump's answer was to acquire a founding team with compute-trading experience — meaning no mature talent pool exists yet. The first wave of roles will most likely go to three backgrounds: power or commodity derivatives, crypto perpetual market making, and capacity pricing inside data centers or cloud providers.
