China Quant Talent Market Whitepaper 2026: Beyond ¥3 Trillion, Hiring the Right People

2026-09-13 · Ken Zhang / Gravitas Recruitment

The quant industry hit a curious contradiction in 2026: AUM climbing to record highs, while the people doing the hiring grow more anxious by the month. Quant long-only equity AUM reached ¥1.83 trillion in H1 and China’s quant industry passed ¥3 trillion — yet average excess return on index-enhancement products fell to 3.11%, nearly 80% below a year earlier.

AUM Up, Alpha Down

On the scale side: quant long-only equity AUM hit ¥1.83 trillion in H1, up ¥1.1 trillion in six months; 73 quant funds now manage over ¥10bn, close to half of all large private funds.

On the return side: average excess return on index-enhancement products was 3.11%, against 14.17% a year earlier. After a sharp July drawdown (quant long-only products fell 16.94% on average), analysts describe the industry as shifting from a speed race to a knockout race.

What Firms Screen For Has Changed

Bigger AUM and thinner alpha are rewriting hiring criteria. The question used to be how many factors a candidate can produce; now it is whether they can explain the financial logic behind a factor, recalibrate models in extreme markets, and engineer strategies into production.

“Research taste” is the phrase of the year. When a large model can generate hundreds of factors overnight, the scarce skill is no longer producing — it is screening and falsifying: spotting look-ahead bias and flawed logic in AI-generated strategies.

Three Shifts Under Way

AI-convergent talent is at roughly a 1:4 supply-demand ratio, with a shortfall above 30% for mid-to-senior researchers. 42% of financial-engineering graduates now choose quant roles, yet fewer than 10% have genuine deep-integration capability.

Shanghai is the undisputed centre, taking about 57% of placed offers. Of 31 wholly foreign-owned private fund managers, 26 are based in Shanghai — Bridgewater (China) manages over ¥60bn and Two Sigma’s onshore entity became the second foreign fund to cross ¥10bn this year. In Hong Kong, Jane Street, Jump, IMC and SIG are all expanding office space and teams.

On pay, the ratio is what matters: median base at foreign firms is about 1.7x Chinese firms, and Hong Kong is about 1.9x the mainland. Development and engineering roles have now caught up with research roles in placement mix.

Three Rules for Candidates

One to three years into a career is the golden window to move.

Low-latency stacks (C++, CUDA, KDB+) combined with LLM engineering are the highest-premium combination right now.

In negotiation, a guaranteed bonus and a next-year bonus commitment usually matter more than the base number.
