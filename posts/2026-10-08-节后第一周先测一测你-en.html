First Week Back: Test How Far You Are From a Quant Offer

2026-10-08 · Ken Zhang / Gravitas Recruitment

The seven-day holiday is over. You are back at your desk today, and 2026 has less than three months left on the clock.

(One reminder first: this Saturday, 10 October, is a make-up workday — don't forget to set the alarm.)

Q4 is the tightest stretch of the quant recruiting year. Q3 scorecards have just landed, firms are setting next year's headcount, HR desks are releasing roles one after another, and interview windows concentrate between mid-October and December. Which means the next two weeks are the best value-for-effort moment to send out your CV — drag it any later and the process crosses into next year.

So today, no market talk. A more practical question instead: if you had to interview this week, where do you actually stand?

The Quantopia self-test site (quantquiz.cn) has two things. Done together they take under 20 minutes — baseline first, then act.

The first: the Quant Interview Gauntlet. Ten questions drawn at random from a 60-question bank, different every time you open it. Not a brain-teaser collection — these are high-frequency exam points from the front line of overseas quant, the kind that can screen out whether you have actually done the work. Four dimensions: mathematical intuition, systems fundamentals, market understanding, decision-making. Aim to finish within 10 minutes, no looking things up. You get a result card at the end: total score, how many peers you beat, per-dimension scores, and full explanations — where you went wrong and what each question actually tests, line by line.

Its most useful output is not the score. It is the four-dimension bar chart — whichever dimension visibly sags is the hole your CV most needs filled. A combination of 90 for mathematical intuition and 0 for systems fundamentals tells you far more than a total of 30.

The second: Which Quant Are You. Eight questions, MBTI-style. You get a result card: your type, its rarity in percent — plus a hidden type you may or may not hit, depending on luck. The type you test as may match the version you give in self-introductions. Or diverge a little.

It looks like play, but it is directly usable in interviews. You have 90 seconds to explain what kind of player you are — signal-mining bent, systems-building bent, risk-controlling bent — and you need a formulation to start from.

The two entry points:

Interview Gauntlet → quantquiz.cn/interview.html

Which Quant Are You → quantquiz.cn/persona.html

When you're done, don't close the page — screenshot the card. When you are actually in the interview room, look back and see how accurate it was.

By the way, report in the comments: which dimension scored lowest for you? Just the number — no explanation needed.
